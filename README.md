# hospital-resources-management
 A project that  will make a major change in the health-care 
 In modern health care systems, effective management of hospital resources is a central column in ensuring high quality patient care, especially during increased demand of seasonal disease such as outbreaks of seasonal disease. One of the most consistent and estimated examples of such growth is the annual flu season, which can greatly stress the hospital infrastructure, especially intensive care units (ICUs), emergency departments and staffing capabilities. Influenza epidemics can lead to a rapid increase in the patient's entry, which requires immediate or intensive care, causing the hospital's capacity to be heavy if resources are not already allocated.
 
The project examines and analyses a data-driven approach for hospital resource management by imitating and analysing operating data from 100 hospitals during the 12-week flu season. The dataset includes major matrix including total beds, occupied beds, ICU beds and their use, level of staff and counting of weekly flu case. By synthesizing these variables, the project tries to highlight the pattern in the hospital, assesses the relationship

The study is first structured to provide searching insight to how hospitals are affected during the flu season, then machine learning to predict ICU demand on the basis of flu activity - especially linear regression - apply. The purpose of this future is the purpose of supporting initial-warning systems for hospital administrators, allowing them to continuously score resources. Finally, by analyzing the staff-to-bed ratio along with the rates, the project proposes data-supported strategies for the workforce allocation, which is often one of the most constrained and difficult resources to raise in a short deadline
